# Speaker notes · The Time Machine

> ภาษาอังกฤษง่ายระดับ B1-B2 · ไม่ต้องท่องจำ ใช้คำของตัวเองได้ · ดู keyword แล้วพูดต่อเอง
> ประมาณ 30-40 วินาทีต่อสไลด์ รวมราว 12 นาที

---

**01 · Cover**
Say: Hi, I'm Dan. Today I'll show my design for The Time Machine app.
Keywords: booking app · any time and place · safe

**02 · Overview**
Say: First I'll talk about the problem, then my design choices, and at the end what I would do next.
Keywords: problem → choices → next steps

**03 · What's the problem?**
Say: People want to visit the past or the future, but they can make mistakes or land in danger. Most of them don't know much history.
Keywords: mistakes · danger · little history

**04 · The brief**
Say: The app must let people book a slot and pick the exact time and place. The company also wants to promote some trips. The CMS was a bonus.
Keywords: book a slot · no voice · featured trips · CMS bonus

**05 · Insights**
Say: I didn't have time to talk to users, so I used desk research. Three things guided me.
Keywords: voice = wrong place · too many confirms = people stop reading · simple risk levels

**06 · Hypothesis**
Say: My idea is simple. If people use clear pickers and the app checks safety for them, they will book the right trip.
Keywords: visual pickers · automatic safety check · fewer wrong bookings

**07 · How I worked**
Say: I only had four hours, so I chose one main journey and did it well. I cut extra features first.
Keywords: scope · research · flow · wireframes · design system · UI

**08 · User flow**
Say: The journey has six steps, from planning to coming back home. The two orange steps are where safety matters most.
Keywords: plan · check · book · depart · in-trip · return

**09 · Full flow and edge cases**
Say: This is the full flow. The main path goes from left to right. The red lines show what happens when something goes wrong, like a blocked time or a danger alert.
Keywords: main path · red = problems · blocked · danger alert · reminders

**10 · No booking mistakes**
Say: Instead of voice, people pick BC or AD, then year, month and day. A summary sentence always shows where they will land, so they can check it.
Keywords: BC/AD · local time · summary sentence · hold to confirm

**11 · Safety first**
Say: The app checks every trip by itself. Safe trips go ahead. Caution trips show a warning. Blocked trips can't be booked, and the app offers safer times.
Keywords: safe · caution · blocked · icon + word + colour

**12 · Help people choose**
Say: Many users don't know history, so Assist asks two quick questions and suggests only safe trips.
Keywords: two questions · safe ideas · one-line reason

**13 · No dark patterns**
Say: The business wants to share data with tour agencies. I made it opt-in and never ticked by default. People can still book without sharing. I think this builds trust.
Keywords: clear "Featured" label · opt-in · PDPA · trust

**14 · During the trip**
Say: During the trip, the most important things are time left and the pick-up point. The watch shows them at a glance, so you don't need to take out a phone in 1889.
Keywords: countdown · reminders 30/10/2 · danger alert · watch

**15 · CMS for the team**
Say: Staff can see who is travelling and who needs help. People in danger go to the top, with a Recall button.
Keywords: traveler monitor · alerts on top · recall · safety check before publish

**16 · How I would measure it**
Say: These numbers are goals, not results. I would test them with real users.
Keywords: wrong bookings · blocked trips · time to book

**17 · How I organised it**
Say: The Figma file has three pages. All screens use the same components, so it's easy to update.
Keywords: 3 pages · 17 colour variables · 22 components · auto layout

**18 · How I used AI**
Say: I led the design. I decided the scope, the flow and the style, and I designed the first UI screen myself. Then AI helped me build the other screens from my flow, notes and research. I checked and fixed every screen.
Keywords: me = scope, flow, style, first screen, checking · AI = build the rest, components, images

**19 · What's next**
Say: The biggest gap is that I haven't tested it with users yet. Next, I'd like to test the summary sentence and how people feel about sharing data.
Keywords: no user test yet · test summary · test data sharing · accessibility

**20 · Thank you**
Say: Thank you for listening. I'm happy to answer any questions.
